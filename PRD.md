# Meal.OS Product Overview

## Summary

Meal.OS is an AI meal operating system for Indian households that coordinate meal planning, cook handoff, and shopping in one workflow.

The product is built around a simple operating loop:

1. capture tonight's kitchen context,
2. generate tomorrow's meal options,
3. approve one plan,
4. send clear execution outputs to the kitchen.

## Primary Users

- households planning the next day's meals
- cooks who benefit from a clear brief or voice-note handoff

## Product Goals

- reduce nightly decision fatigue
- make household food preferences operational
- reduce waste through leftover-aware suggestions
- improve shopping accuracy with delta lists
- reduce kitchen coordination friction

## Core Outputs

- plan options
- cook brief
- voice-note handoff
- shopping delta

## Product Principles

- plan for real households, not idealized kitchen routines
- make the AI explain its suggestions through visible structure
- keep the daily interaction short
- turn meal planning into execution, not just inspiration
